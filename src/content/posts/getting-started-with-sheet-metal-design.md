Sheet-metal design becomes much easier when manufacturability is treated as a constraint from the first sketch, not as a final inspection step.

## Start with the process

Before modeling, identify the material, thickness, bend method, and likely tooling. Those decisions establish the bend radius, relief strategy, and minimum flange sizes that the part can support.

## Build around a reliable flat pattern

Use a clear base flange and keep bends consistent wherever possible. A predictable feature tree makes design changes safer and gives fabrication teams a flat pattern they can trust.

## Validate the handoff

Check the unfolded part, inspect bend notes, and confirm that the exported profile preserves the intended scale. Good sheet-metal CAD is not only about the 3D shape; it is about the quality of the next operation.
